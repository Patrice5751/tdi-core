# Trading Decision Intelligence (TDI)

**Version:** 0.9 (Work in Progress)

---

# Mission

Trading Decision Intelligence (TDI) is an objective decision-support system designed for discretionary traders.

Its mission is **not** to predict the market.

Its mission is **not** to replace the trader.

Its mission is to improve the quality, consistency and discipline of trading decisions through objective, explainable and measurable analysis.

The final decision always belongs to the trader.

---

# Vision

TDI aims to become a professional decision-support platform capable of:

- objectively analysing a trading opportunity;
- evaluating its technical quality;
- explaining every score transparently;
- identifying strengths and weaknesses;
- assessing risk quality;
- improving decision consistency;
- learning from historical trading performance.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Objectivity

Every trade is evaluated using predefined and reproducible rules.

No discretionary bias.

---

## 2. Explainability

Every score, recommendation and verdict must be fully explainable.

No black-box decisions.

TDI must always be able to justify every recommendation it produces.

---

## 3. Risk First

Capital preservation always has priority over profit.

An attractive technical setup with poor risk management must never receive a high recommendation.

---

## 4. Continuous Improvement

TDI evolves through measurable improvements.

Every modification must provide an objective benefit.

Ideas are implemented only if they improve decision quality.

---

## 5. Statistical Validation

No idea is considered correct because it appears convincing.

Every improvement must be validated on a statistically significant sample of real trades.

Facts always prevail over opinions.

---

# Trader Philosophy

TDI never replaces the trader.

TDI assists the trader.

The trader remains responsible for:

- market context;
- execution;
- psychology;
- discipline;
- final decision.

---

# Development Philosophy

TDI is developed according to professional software engineering practices:

- Test Driven Development (TDD)
- Clean Architecture
- Modular Design
- Single Responsibility Principle
- Continuous Refactoring
- Explainable AI principles

Every new feature must remain:

- useful;
- testable;
- maintainable;
- measurable.

---

# Success Criteria

TDI will be considered successful only if, over hundreds of real trades, it demonstrates:

- better decision quality;
- increased consistency;
- improved risk management;
- measurable improvement in trading performance.

---

# Long-Term Objective

Build one of the world's most transparent, objective and explainable decision-support systems for discretionary trading.

The official project language is English.

Source code, documentation and reports are written in English.
