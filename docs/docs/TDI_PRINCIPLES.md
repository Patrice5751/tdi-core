# TDI Principles

Version 1.0 Draft

---

# Mission

Better decisions before better profits.

TDI exists to improve the quality and consistency of trading decisions.

---

# Principle 1

The trader always decides.

TDI never executes trades.

TDI analyses, explains and recommends.

---

# Principle 2

Every recommendation must be explainable.

No black-box decisions.

Every score, recommendation and warning must be justified.

---

# Principle 3

Market structure comes first.

Indicators confirm.

Indicators never replace market structure.

---

# Principle 4

Risk management is mandatory.

A technically good setup can still be rejected because of poor risk management.

---

# Principle 5

The software follows the trading methodology.

The methodology never adapts to the software.

---

# Principle 6

Every important rule must be testable.

If a rule cannot be tested, it should not become part of TDI.

---

# Principle 7

Development is evidence-driven.

Ideas become features only after experimental validation.

---

# Principle 8

Architecture must remain modular.

Every Engine has one responsibility.

Every Specification has one responsibility.

Every Result object has one responsibility.

---

# Principle 9

Backward compatibility.

New versions should preserve existing behaviour unless an intentional change is documented.

---

# Principle 10

Continuous improvement.

TDI is never considered finished.

Every version should improve decision quality while preserving explainability.
